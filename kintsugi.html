Kintsugi: The Art of Embracing Imperfection

by Butterfly

A conversation with a friend...

The vase lay shattered on the floor.

"I am sorry," she said as the both of us sat there in silence.

I got a bowl and mixed a paste of gold. The both of us attempted to mend it.

"It wouldn't look the same," she cried.

"It doesn't have to be," I replied.

"But it was your favourite... why don't you just buy a new vase."

"It still wouldn't be the same."

"The gold would show the cracks."

"The gold would highlight its beauty."

"But it was priceless."

"And it still is."

"Thank you...."

"For what?"

"For uh... forgiving me."

We finished mending the vase.

She turned away and walked towards the door.

"Wait... take it. I believe that it would be more valuable to you than anyone else."

Butterfly
