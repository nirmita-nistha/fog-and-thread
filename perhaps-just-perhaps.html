Perhaps, Just Perhaps

by Samyuktha

read
← back to threadbare
✦

Maybe she is overthinking,
Maybe she is overreacting,
Or maybe, just maybe,
She is the one at fault.

But why did it hurt so much
When she read all those messages,
All those theories about her,
When she was right there,
Right there for you to ask?

Maybe it was the trust she had in you,
Or the little hope she held on to,
The hope you broke in a second,
Without even thinking it through.

Is it so wrong that she trusted you
With her secrets and her feelings,
Only for you to make her feel
Like they never mattered enough to you?

The excruciating pain she felt
As she read every single word,
The way she hid it all behind a smile,
Pretending none of it had hurt.

How do you expect her to act normal
After everything that she had read?
How was she supposed to look at you the same
With all those words still in her head?

Now, she can't even let herself be sad,
Not without wondering what you'll think,
So from now on, she'll act like everything's fine,
She'll wear a smile and say she's okay,
Just like you asked her to.

Because being sad isn't easy anymore,
Not when her feelings might be misunderstood,
Not when something as simple as being hurt
Can turn into something she has to explain.

Maybe she is overthinking,
Maybe she is overreacting,
Or maybe, just maybe,
she is simply hurt by someone she trusted.

And perhaps the saddest part of it all
Isn't that you made her question herself,
But that you made her feel
Like she had to hide the pain
Just to keep everything else okay.

Samyuktha
