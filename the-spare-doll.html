Poetry

the spare doll

by Tharunika

read
← back to threadbare
✦

she was the doll on the shelf
not unwanted enough to throw away
but not wanted enough to be taken home

when the hands came searching
she would hope, and hope, and hope
maybe today would be the day
they'd pick her without a second thought.

But they wouldn't.

She held their tears in her tiny hands,
be there for them till their hurt turned small.
And when their heart was full and they found another doll,
she was returned to the shelf
as though she'd meant nothing at all.

She never understood how
she could be everyone's somewhere to go
but nobody's place to stay.
How they could trust her with their saddest secrets
yet forget her in their happiest moments.
How she could know the smallest things about everyone,
while nobody noticed her falling apart.

She never asked for much.
Just once, to be someone's first thought, not someone's replacement,
to be missed when she was not there
to be searched for in a crowded room
the way she searched for everyone, everywhere.

But wanting, she learnt, was a terrible mistake,
for a doll never chosen.
So she swallowed every ache,
and every little pain,
and let the silence consume her instead.

Sometimes she wondered,
if she was made wrong.
Was there something under the porcelain surface that she was missing?
some little piece everyone else possessed,
a piece that made them worth coming back for?

She would watch the other dolls get plucked away from the shelves,
carried away gently in someone's arms
and simply tell herself,
she isn't meant for that kind of love.

Perhaps she was made to be temporary,
a passing warmth,
a place to rest, when the world got cruel
and to leave, when someone better came along.

Slowly, she learnt to stop looking towards the door.

Because hope had became the cruelest thing she knew.

And when the shop grew quiet,
and dust settled into her plastic lashes,
she sat exactly where she was before,
waiting for someone to want her
the way she had always wanted them.

Tharunika
