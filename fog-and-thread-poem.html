Fog and Thread

by Butterfly

read
← back to threadbare
✦

I stood in the middle of nowhere,
The fog clouding my thoughts.
I thought I saw someone,
But it was an illusion.

I walked around,
Looking for water,
Looking for a path,
Looking for a friend.

The crippling anxiety,
Overrid my mind,
And so did the fear,
Of not being loved.

But a thread indeed,
Does connect us all,
And in the fog,
I saw a strange figure.

It was her.
The one who always found me,
When I got lost.

She was the path,
The solution to my problems,
The light to my 'darkness'.
She was home.

Well,
They do say,
"When the fog confuses you,
The thread guides you home."

Butterfly
